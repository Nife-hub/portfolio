An AI-powered study assistant

The problem: Long documents can be overwhelming to study and difficult to turn into focused revision material.

The solution: Upload text, a PDF, a DOCX file, or an article link. StudyMode uses the Claude API to create chunk-by-chunk summaries, flashcards, and questions, helping readers work through the material one piece at a time.
